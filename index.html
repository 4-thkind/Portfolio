Research

ICML 2026

Adaptive Termination in Agentic Multimodal RAG via Cross-Model Evidence Grounding

International Conference on Machine Learning — ~26% global acceptance rate — Published as a first-year undergraduate

Most retrieval-augmented systems retrieve greedily or stop arbitrarily — they don't know when they know enough. This paper introduces two contributions: CMEGS (Cross-Model Evidence Grounding Score), a claim-level grounding metric that measures whether retrieved evidence actually supports each assertion; and MIR (Minimum Information Requirement), a principled stopping criterion that gives a multimodal agentic RAG pipeline a mathematically grounded sense of sufficiency.

Projects

Risk ML · Fintech · India

KAVACH

A defence-only risk manager for Indian D2C merchants, who lose 5 to 15% of gross margin to COD return abuse, coordinated rings and chargebacks. Abuse is invisible per-order, so KAVACH pairs a calibrated XGBoost return-risk scorer with NetworkX graph detection over shared phones, devices, VPAs and fuzzy-matched addresses. That catches structure a per-row model cannot see. Ring detection scored 0.889 precision at 1.000 recall on held-out data. Every decision ships with exact SHAP attributions and a SHA-256 hash-chained audit trail.

Agentic AI · Privacy · India

NETRĀ

An AI growth copilot for Indian Kirana stores, 90% of a $600B+ grocery market running blind on demand. The hard part is that naive market intelligence enables competitor surveillance and price collusion. So NETRĀ enforces a five-layer privacy boundary: k-anonymity cohort gating (N ≥ 10) with adaptive radius expansion, Laplace differential privacy, and an anti-reconstruction interceptor that blocks differencing attacks. Demand forecasting, non-collusive price benchmarking and credit scoring reach merchants over Soundbox audio, WhatsApp and web in 7 Indic languages.

Hybrid ML · In-Browser

ATLASS

A hybrid recommender that runs entirely in the browser, with no inference server and no round-trip latency. Rating a film projects it into a 32-dimensional taste vector by SVD fold-in, blended 70/30 with a 48-dimensional LSA content profile so recommendations stay coherent when rating data is sparse. Pre-trained weight matrices ship as ~700KB of JSON and re-score the entire feed in under 50ms. Built on MovieLens (9,742 films, 100,836 ratings) and fronted by a WebGL gallery rendering cards along a parametric arc.

AI Shopping Agent · E-commerce

SCOUT

Shopping across Indian platforms means a dozen tabs and no real answer on price or delivery. SCOUT collapses that into one conversational query, searching Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, Myntra and Ajio in parallel. Claude routes intent and pulls typed constraints like budget, brand, features and pincode out of natural Hindi or Hinglish, including slang and typos. Results are de-duplicated by title fingerprint, then ranked on a transparent six-factor score across price fit, ratings, review volume, delivery speed, platform trust and feature match, with pros and cons streamed token-by-token over SSE.
